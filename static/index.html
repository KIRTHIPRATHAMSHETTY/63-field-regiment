ACADEMIC METHODOLOGY & VIVA GUIDE

1. Why Random Forest Classifier?

Random Forest handles non-linear feature interactions (such as mobility × terrain penalty and fog intel imbalances) without risk of overfitting on tabular wargaming simulations.

2. How is Synthetic Data Generated?

Physics-based simulation rules model force strength ratios, logistics depletion, terrain modifiers, and weather visibility before applying Gaussian noise (\(\sigma=0.05\)) to produce 20,000 realistic scenarios.

3. What is SHAP Explainability?

SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) uses game-theoretic Shapley values to calculate the exact marginal contribution of each input variable to the model's outcome prediction.
